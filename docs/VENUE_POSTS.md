## 1. Chapala.com Webboard — reply in the existing licence thread

> A few things that tripped me up getting my Jalisco license in Guadalajara recently, in case it helps anyone here:
>
> 1. The written exam is 20 questions pulled from an official bank of 103 — you need 16 right, and if you fail you wait 15 days to retake.
> 2. The computer exam is Spanish-only, but you can ask for a paper English version at the exam window. You can also bring an interpreter.
> 3. Since 2025 most offices use a driving **simulator** instead of the on-road test. Exaggerate your mirror checks — the machine scores head movement.
>
> I went through the whole 103-question bank while studying and ended up building a free bilingual practice site around it — improved English translations side-by-side with the official Spanish, plus the exact government English phrasing you'll see on test day: https://getexpatdrive.com/countries/mexico/jalisco/
>
> No account, no cost — I built it because the existing resources were scattered and outdated. If anyone takes the exam soon and notices a question has changed, tell me and I'll fix it the same day.
